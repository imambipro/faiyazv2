Hindus After Hasina

S M Faiyaz Hossain & Anindya Banerjee

A documented account of what changed for Bangladesh's Hindu minority after a political transition. Built on field reports, interviews, and public records, the book asks a simple question with difficult answers: who protects a minority when the state steps back?
