About Faiyaz

Researcher. Writer. Observer of the spaces between policy and people.

S M Faiyaz Hossain is a Sydney-based researcher and writer whose work focuses on South Asian politics, democratic change, and the rights of religious and ethnic minorities.

His writing combines field research with close reading of public records and media. He is interested in what happens to vulnerable communities during political transitions, and in how those stories are told, or not told, across borders.

He is the co-author, with journalist Anindya Banerjee, of Hindus After Hasina. He writes essays, speaks at academic and community events, and contributes commentary to international media.
